# Image Director privacy policy

Publisher: Prabal. Effective November 6, 2026.

Image Director is a package of creative workflow instructions and icon assets. It has no publisher-operated server, analytics, tracking, or account system. Prabal does not collect, receive, retain, or share your prompts, reference images, or generated images through this package.

The host application and any image-generation, editing, search, or other tools you choose process your content under their own policies and settings. This package does not change those arrangements. Requests to access or delete data held by those providers must go to the relevant provider.

Support is provided through this repository's public GitHub Issues. Information you voluntarily post there is public and handled by GitHub under its policies; do not post confidential images, credentials, or personal information. You can edit your own issue content using GitHub's controls. For support or privacy questions, open an issue at https://github.com/Prabal2004/image-director/issues.
